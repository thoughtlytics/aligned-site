Privacy Policy

Aligned by Thoughtlytics · Last updated: November 14, 2026

This policy explains what the Aligned add-on for Google Sheets and this website do with information. The short version: Aligned works inside your own spreadsheet, and we do not collect, store or sell your goals, tasks or notes.

1. What Aligned accesses

When you use Aligned, it works only with the spreadsheet you are using it in. It creates and edits the tabs it needs (Goals, Routines, Planner, Today, Archive and so on) and reads them to build your daily list. It also shows menus and dialog boxes in the sheet. This is why it asks for permission to access the current spreadsheet and to show its user interface.

That information stays in your spreadsheet, in your Google account. It is not sent to us or to any third party. Aligned has no account system and no sign-in, and it does not connect to any outside server.

2. Small settings stored by Google

Aligned keeps two small settings using Google’s own storage for add-ons: the date you last ran Process Day (saved with your spreadsheet), and whether you have already seen the optional feedback box (saved with your Google account). They are not sent to us.

3. Optional feedback form

Aligned may show a one-time box inviting you to fill in a short Google Form about what would make a future Pro version worth paying for. Filling it in is optional. If you choose to, the form collects your answers and, only if you type it, your email address. We use this to understand what people need and, if you gave your email, to send you occasional updates about Aligned. We do not sell or share it. The form is run on Google Forms and is also subject to Google’s privacy policy.

4. This website

This website is a simple set of static pages. It does not use cookies, advertising or analytics. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages and its domain is managed with Cloudflare, so those services may process technical information such as your IP address when you visit, under their own privacy policies.

5. Google API Services User Data Policy

Aligned’s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6. Earlier versions

Earlier versions of Aligned asked you to create an account on this website. That account system has been discontinued and is no longer used.

7. Sharing and security

We do not sell your information and we do not share it with third parties for advertising. Because your spreadsheet data never reaches us, the only personal information we may hold is what you choose to give us through the feedback form or by emailing us.

8. Your choices and deleting your data

  • To stop using Aligned, uninstall it from the Google Workspace Marketplace (or Extensions → Add-ons → Manage add-ons). You can also delete the Aligned tabs from your sheet at any time.
  • To remove anything you sent through the feedback form, or to stop receiving updates, email us at support@thoughtlytics.com and we will delete it.

9. Children

Aligned is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page. If a change affects how we handle your information, we will make that clear in the add-on or on this site.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy: support@thoughtlytics.com.
