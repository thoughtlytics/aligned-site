Turn your goals into daily action, inside Google Sheets

Aligned is a free planner that connects your long-term goals to what you do today. No account, no sign-in. Your data stays in your own spreadsheet.

Get Aligned for Google Sheets How it works

Free. Works with Google Sheets.

How it works

  1. Set up once. Open any Google Sheet, go to Extensions → Aligned → How to start, and click one button. Aligned builds your planner and fills it with sample rows.
  2. Add your goals and routines. List what you want to achieve, then add habits and recurring work with simple days like “Mon, Thu” or “Weekdays”. Add one-off tasks with a date.
  3. Each morning, Sync Today. Your Today tab fills with routines and planner tasks, including anything overdue.
  4. Each evening, Process Day. Finished tasks move to your Archive and unfinished ones roll to tomorrow.

What’s inside

GoalsWrite down what you want to achieve. Every task can point back to a goal.
RoutinesHabits and recurring tasks, with optional start and end dates.
PlannerOne-off tasks for a specific date. Unfinished tasks wait for you.
Today & ArchiveWork from one clean list. See what you finished, day by day.

Free, and private by design

Aligned works entirely inside your Google Sheet. It reads and writes only the spreadsheet you use it in, and your goals and tasks are never sent to us. Read the privacy policy.
